# Borrower Copilot rules

These are explainable defaults for a take-home assignment, not claims about a specific lender.

| Rule | Value / threshold | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard lender FOIR for salaried personal borrowing | 55% | A lender-style upper capacity estimate for stable documented income | my judgement |
| Standard lender FOIR for self-employed personal borrowing | 50% | Allows for more income variability and documentation friction | my judgement |
| Standard lender FOIR for informal/gig income | 42% | Adds conservatism for volatile or less documentable income | my judgement |
| Safe FOIR starting point | 40% | Separates borrower safety from a lender’s maximum | my judgement |
| Dependent living-cost adjustment | ₹5,000 per dependent/month | Keeps the safe capacity from treating all post-EMI income as flexible | my judgement |
| Income stress scenario | 20% income reduction | Tests whether the plan has resilience beyond the average month | my judgement |
| Default tenure | 48 months | Provides a useful comparison point without claiming an optimal tenure | my judgement |
| Maximum maturity age | 65 | Prevents an unrealistic full-tenure estimate for older borrowers | my judgement |
| Processing fee estimate | 2% of requested amount | Makes the APR estimate include a visible all-in cost component | my judgement |
| Unknown credit score | Widen rate band by 2.5 percentage points | Unknown is uncertainty, not evidence of poor credit | my judgement |
| Missing essential expenses | Reduce confidence by 20 points | Safe affordability cannot be precise without household outgoings | my judgement |
| Recent repayment stress | Reduce safe capacity by 35% | Recent difficulty is a direct signal that a new EMI may be unsafe | my judgement |
| High income volatility | Reduce safe capacity by 20% | Protects against planning around a strong month | my judgement |
| Emergency savings below three months | Reduce safe capacity by 15% | Leaves room for shocks without immediately using debt | my judgement |

## Product floors used for rate estimates

These are illustrative annual starting rates before profile adjustments:

- Personal loan: 12%
- Secured business loan: 11%
- Loan against property: 9.5%
- Vehicle loan: 10.5%
- Gold loan: 10%

Actual lender pricing varies significantly. The interface should present these as estimated fair bands, never as offers.
